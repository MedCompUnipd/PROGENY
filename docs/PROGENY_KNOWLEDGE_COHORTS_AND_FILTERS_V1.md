# Superseded knowledge cohort policy

This v1 policy required the donor accession to equal the target's external
accession. That restriction was incorrect for targets whose annotations were
attributed through certified M8 accepted donor links. Do not use v1 cohort
artifacts. The current contract and publishing instructions are in
[PROGENY_KNOWLEDGE_COHORTS_AND_FILTERS_V2.md](PROGENY_KNOWLEDGE_COHORTS_AND_FILTERS_V2.md).
